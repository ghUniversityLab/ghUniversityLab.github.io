Led Banner 隐私政策

更新日期：2026年10月20日。运营主体：ganghua chen（App Store Connect 登记名称）；联系邮箱：cghledtech@outlook.com。

1. 本应用与账号

Led Banner 提供弹幕编辑、全屏展示、应援灯光、作品保存和图片/GIF/视频导出。本应用不提供自建账号注册、密码登录或注册码服务。会员购买、订阅和恢复由您的 Apple 账户及 App Store 管理；我们不会获得您的 Apple 账户密码、银行卡号或支付凭证中的完整付款资料。

2. 保存在设备上的内容与权限

您输入的灯牌文字、作品、模板设置及导入照片保存在设备的 App 存储空间。我们的业务统计不上传这些文字、照片、音频或导出文件。系统备份和 iCloud 备份由您的设备设置决定。

选图使用系统照片选择器。照片保存、麦克风和摄像头/闪光灯相关能力仅在使用相应功能时按系统要求申请权限；声音响应使用麦克风分析声音强弱，不用于上传录音。您可在系统设置中管理权限。导出分享由您选择接收应用或服务，接收方按其规则处理文件。

3. 配置、会员和必要网络请求

应用通过 HTTPS 下载经签名验证的产品配置及会员页图片，并通过 StoreKit 查询商品价格、购买记录及会员权益。隐私选择前会发起一次不携带 Cookie、设备标识或业务事件的 HEAD 网络请求，用于完成系统网络访问提示；服务提供方仍可能接收网络通信所必需的 IP 地址。拒绝使用统计不影响这些必要功能。

4. 可选使用统计和诊断

首次使用时，您可以选择是否允许使用统计；不同意也可继续使用并同步会员权益。只有您同意且产品配置允许统计时，应用才初始化友盟统计/性能组件并发送自有业务事件。

自有业务日志可能包含 IDFV、应用安装 UUID、事件会话标识、应用版本与运行环境、所用功能类别和模板编号、会员状态、会员页入口，以及购买套餐、已验证交易金额和币种。用途是了解功能使用、购买转化和改善产品，不包含作品文字、照片、密码、银行卡资料或完整收据。

友盟组件用于使用分析和稳定性诊断，可能处理设备/应用信息、网络信息（包括 IP 地址）、SDK 标识、崩溃和卡顿、启动、内存及页面性能信息。当前集成关闭友盟 ASA/SKAN 归因、进端采集、网络请求内容采集、JavaScript 桥内容采集和应用日志采集。本应用未接入广告投放，也不主动读取 IDFA；当前产品配置未启用 Apple Ads 归因。

5. 第三方服务

  • Apple / App Store：商品、购买、恢复和订阅管理，适用 Apple 隐私政策。
  • 友盟移动统计及 U-APM：运营方为友盟同欣（北京）科技有限公司；用于前述可选分析与性能诊断，适用 友盟隐私政策。
  • GitHub Pages：托管官网、政策、加密配置和部分素材，访问时可能产生服务方的网络访问日志，适用 GitHub 隐私声明。

我们不会将您的灯牌内容或业务统计出售给数据经纪商。第三方自行处理的数据及保存安排以其政策为准。

6. 反馈、保存与您的选择

意见反馈先保存为本地草稿。只有您在系统邮件界面确认发送后，我们才会收到邮件地址、您填写的内容，以及您选择附带的应用/iOS 版本；用于回复问题和提供支持。请不要在反馈中填写密码或完整付款凭据。

本地作品可在应用中管理，卸载应用会移除应用的本地存储；设备备份副本需按系统设置管理。网络数据仅在提供服务、处理请求及履行适用义务所需期间保存。

您可在首次提示中拒绝可选统计，管理系统权限，或停止使用、卸载应用。如需了解、访问、更正、删除已提交的信息，或提出撤回授权请求，请联系上述邮箱并提供核验请求所必需的信息。我们将按适用要求处理；无需提供本应用账号或密码，因为本应用不存在自建账号。

7. 安全与政策更新

网络配置采用 HTTPS、加密和签名校验，购买使用 StoreKit 验证；这些措施不意味着任何系统绝对安全。政策变更会更新本页日期；新增需要授权的数据用途时，会先提供说明并取得所需授权。

Led Banner Privacy Policy

Updated October 20, 2026. Operator: ganghua chen, the legal entity registered in App Store Connect. Contact: cghledtech@outlook.com.

1. The app and accounts

Led Banner provides banner editing, fullscreen displays, cheering lights, saved designs and image/GIF/video exports. It has no app-owned account registration, password login or registration-code service. Apple and the App Store manage membership purchases, subscriptions and restoration. We do not receive your Apple account password, card number or full payment credentials.

2. On-device content and permissions

Your banner text, designs, settings and imported photos are stored in the app's on-device storage. Our business analytics do not upload this text, photos, audio or exported files. System and iCloud backups depend on your device settings.

Photo import uses the system photo picker. Photo saving, microphone and camera/flash capabilities request system permissions when the corresponding feature is used. Sound-responsive effects analyze sound levels without uploading recordings. Manage permissions in system settings. When you share an export, the destination you choose handles it under its own rules.

3. Configuration, membership and necessary requests

The app downloads signed product configuration and membership images over HTTPS, and uses StoreKit to obtain product prices, purchases and entitlements. Before the privacy choice, one HEAD request helps complete the system network-access prompt. It carries no cookies, device identifiers or business events; a network service may still receive the IP address needed for communication. Declining analytics does not disable these necessary functions.

4. Optional analytics and diagnostics

At first use, you can choose whether to allow analytics. You can continue using the app and synchronize membership without consenting. Umeng analytics/performance components and our business-event reporting start only with your consent and an enabled product analytics setting.

Our business logs may contain IDFV, an app installation UUID, event session identifiers, app version and environment, feature categories and template numbers, membership status, membership-page entry points, purchased plans, and verified transaction amounts and currency. These support usage analysis, conversion measurement and product improvements. They do not contain your banner content, photos, passwords, card details or full receipts.

Umeng may process device/app and network information (including IP address), SDK identifiers, crashes, stalls, launch, memory and page-performance data for optional analytics and stability diagnostics. This integration disables Umeng ASA/SKAN attribution, inbound collection, network request-content capture, JavaScript bridge-content capture and application-log capture. The app does not serve ads or actively read IDFA; Apple Ads attribution is disabled in the current product configuration.

5. Service providers

  • Apple / App Store: products, purchases, restoration and subscription management; see Apple's privacy policy.
  • Umeng mobile analytics and U-APM, operated by Umeng Tongxin (Beijing) Technology Co., Ltd.: optional analytics and diagnostics described above; see Umeng's privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages: website, policies, encrypted configuration and some assets; requests may generate provider access logs. See the GitHub privacy statement.

We do not sell your banner content or business analytics to data brokers. Providers' independent processing and retention are governed by their policies.

6. Feedback, retention and choices

Feedback begins as a local draft. We receive your email address, message and any app/iOS version information you choose to include only after you confirm sending in the system mail interface. We use it to respond and provide support. Do not include passwords or full payment credentials.

Manage local designs in the app. Uninstalling removes app-local storage; manage backup copies through system settings. Network data is retained only as long as needed to provide services, handle requests and meet applicable obligations.

You may decline optional analytics at the initial prompt, manage device permissions, stop using or uninstall the app. For information, access, correction, deletion or a request to withdraw authorization, contact the email above with the information needed to verify your request. We handle requests as required. No app account or password is needed because the app does not create its own accounts.

7. Security and updates

Configuration uses HTTPS, encryption and signature verification, and purchases use StoreKit verification. No system is absolutely secure. Changes update the date on this page; new purposes requiring authorization will be explained and the necessary authorization obtained first.
